THE PROJECT

The Story

For more than a century, Rio de Janeiro’s Carnival has been the world’s largest celebration — two million people per day on the streets, and millions more watching the samba schools parade at the Sapucaí.

In 2028, for the first time, India will have an official presence at the parade. India at Brazil’s Carnival 2028 is a project of Experiences do Francês, conducted together with the India-Brazil Chamber of Commerce, honoring 80 years of diplomatic relations between the two countries and taking India’s colours, music and business community to the Avenue.

01 Culture — An authentic Indian presence at the Sapucaí — costumes, music and choreography designed with Brazilian samba schools.
02 Business — A business delegation and premium hospitality platform connecting Indian and Brazilian companies.
03 Diplomacy — A celebration of 80 years of India–Brazil diplomatic relations, with support from institutional partners.

COMMEMORATIVE BOOK · 80 YEARS
A Legacy Beyond Carnival
Brazil and India — A Friendship Between Two Cultures
80 YEARS OF DIPLOMATIC RELATIONS | 1948–2028

As part of the celebrations marking 80 years of diplomatic relations between Brazil and India (1948–2028), the project will produce an exclusive commemorative coffee-table book documenting the remarkable historical, diplomatic, cultural and economic ties between the two nations.

The publication will also celebrate the extraordinary encounter between Indian culture and Rio de Janeiro's Carnival, including the research, creative development and artistic expression behind a proposed India-themed samba school parade in 2028.

Featuring historical narratives, photography, cultural insights, diplomatic milestones and the stories of people and institutions that have strengthened bilateral relations, this premium hardcover edition will serve as a lasting tribute to the friendship between Brazil and India.

SPONSORSHIP — Where the investment goes
The project’s budget is fully allocated to three fronts: the parade sponsorship at the samba school, the hospitality platform at the camarote, and the experience that surrounds the guests.
The parade sponsorship — the artistic sponsorship of the school — represents US$ 1,427,273 (48.7% of the budget). The camarote, the project’s hospitality operation, represents US$ 1,090,909 (37.2%). The experience — travel, taxes, the commemorative book and fees — represents US$ 413,727 (14.1%).
Brand at the parade — The sponsor’s identity present at India’s historic first parade at the Sapucaí.
Hospitality platform — Four nights of premium hospitality for the sponsor’s guests and clients.
Institutional positioning — Association with the 80 years of India–Brazil diplomatic relations, and presence in the Commemorative Book.
Incentive Law advantage — Part of the investment may be eligible under Brazil’s culture incentive laws (Lei Rouanet / incentive laws), reducing the effective cost for companies. Details on request.
